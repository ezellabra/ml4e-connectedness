\section*{Statement on the use of AI tools}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Statement on the use of AI tools}
The authors used AI tools in the following ways during the preparation of this paper. Claude Deep Research (Anthropic) was used to search for and identify sources and data. Claude Code (Anthropic) was used to write the code that produced the estimations, figures and tables, to typeset the paper in LaTeX, and to draft parts of the text under the authors' direction. The research questions, the structure of the paper, the main modeling decisions, the Discussion and the Conclusion are the work of the authors. The authors reviewed and revised all drafted text, agree with its content, and take full responsibility for the paper.
