\section*{Statement on the use of AI tools}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Statement on the use of AI tools}
The authors used AI tools in the following ways during the preparation of this paper.
Claude Deep Research (Anthropic) was used to search for and identify sources and data.
Claude Code (Anthropic) was used to write the code that produced the estimations,
figures and tables, and to typeset the paper in LaTeX. The research questions, the
structure of the paper and the analysis and interpretation of the results are the
work of the authors. The text of the paper, and in particular the research questions,
the Discussion and the Conclusion, was written by the authors; Claude Code was used
to convert this text into LaTeX format and for minor language editing. Claude (Anthropic) was also used to review a complete draft of the paper; the revisions that followed from that review (the idiosyncratic spillover share, the pure-factor benchmark, the additional robustness checks and bootstrap intervals, and the corresponding passages of text) were drafted with its assistance, implemented with Claude Code, and checked and approved by the authors.
The authors
reviewed all outputs and take full responsibility for the content of the paper.
